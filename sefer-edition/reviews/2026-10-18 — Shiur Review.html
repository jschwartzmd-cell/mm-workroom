2026-10-18 — אל אדון: The Silent Angels, the Hefsek Debate, and Dancing on Shabbos

Content-based review · checked against the PRIMARY Sofer.ai transcript (no timestamps, so passages are cited by quoted phrase, shown as [“…”]) · the Plaud transcript and summary were used only as secondary checks · reviewer also read the current English write-up and the last Sefaria log entries · 18 October 2026 · 7 Cheshvan 5787 · reviewed on the first complete draft, before the corrections listed in Section 5 were applied

1.The Shiur in One Paragraph

A short, early-starting shiur (the Rav had to speak at 10:30 at the home of Rav Alex Edelman) that moves from the end of last week's נשמת into the first berachah before Shema, יוצר אור. After the dedication (the Feffer and Singer families) and a few loose ends from last week (the Rokeach on antiquity; Shimon Kefa; the "thank you, priest, for your sermon" joke; Rav Chaim Vital on נשמת as a substitute for tefillin on Shabbos), he contrasts the weekday אל ברוך גדול דעה, one word per letter, with the twenty-two-line אל אדון על כל המעשים (and the unison-versus-alternating singing custom, with the women joke). The long answer to "why is Shabbos longer" is תוספות סנהדרין ל"ז: six wings, one song each weekday, none left for Shabbos, so Klal Yisrael sings. Hence הכל יודוך: the angels, created on Monday, had a wing in reserve on the first Shabbos. Then the machloket: Rav Saadia Gaon and the Rambam say no additions; the Tur (Toledo) and Rabbeinu Yehuda ben Barzilai (ספר העתים, "a hefsek, a chutzpah") object; Rashi in ספר הפרדס says the Anshei Knesses HaGedolah enacted them. The nafka minah is the Or Zarua read by the Magen Avraham: make up the piyutim after Shemoneh Esrei, "and nobody does." From לאל אשר שבת he moves to ending an aliyah on a דבר טוב (the Maor VaShemesh's לאשמה בה custom; תחש/מעכה in the Imrei Noam), to the Zohar's picture of the angels receiving their missions and dancing on the first Shabbos with Adam HaRishon, to the Misnagdim/Chassidim fight over dancing and the Noda BiYehuda's grandson at the Talner Rebbe's tish, and finally to Rav Moshe Wolfson's niggun for הכל יודוך and the Rokeach.

3.Content Flags — What Was Wrong, Mis-Attributed, Omitted, or Added in the First Draft

Everything below was tested against the Sofer text. All of it is applied in Section 5's list.

Flag 1 — The abstract overstated who calls the piyutim a hefsek. The first draft said Rav Saadia Gaon, the Rambam, the Tur and Rabbeinu Yehuda ben Barzilai "call them a hefsek." The Sofer text has only the last of these using that argument: [“Rabbeinu Yehuda the son of Barzilai … it's a mistake to say it! It's an hefsek”]. Rav Saadia Gaon and the Rambam simply do not include the additions ([“They said the regular nusach of chol”]), and the Tur reports Toledo's omission. Applied: the abstract (English and Hebrew) now says "do not say them" for the first three.

Flag 2 — The "A participant asks about ה' שפתי תפתח" attribution was wrong. In the Sofer the Rav himself asks the room: [“Do you have a general understanding that things that you put in that are like השם שפתי תפתח, they don't become an hefsek, because it's really part of the same ideas? Only the Gemara can say that, not me and you.”] The first draft turned this into a participant's question. Applied.

Flag 3 — The hosafot exchange had the wrong speaker. The first draft had "the Sefardim, a participant says, also make hosafot." The Sofer has the Rav asking [“Sefardim, Sefardim also make hosafot? Okay, some places, in the real אשכנזישע שול'ס, ממש קיין הוספות”]. Applied: it is now the Rav's question and the Rav's statement about Ashkenazi shuls.

Flag 4 — "In other parts of davening" was an invented gloss. The first draft had the Rav say "thank God we don't say them in other parts of davening." The Sofer reads [“and thank God we don't say them because otherwise davening would take much longer”] with no stated location. Applied.

Tension A — "Klal Yisrael" on the first Shabbos. The Rav says [“That very first שבת, כלל ישראל said shirah with the מלאכים”], which is an anachronism on its face (Klal Yisrael did not yet exist), and the Zohar passage later in the shiur has Adam HaRishon joining. The Plaud summary quietly changes it to "humanity." The write-up keeps the Rav's wording but did not mark the tension. Applied: a parenthetical pointer to Section VIII.

Omission B — The practical halachah in the Mishnah Berurah. The Rav says the Magen Avraham requires making up all the piyutim and that "the Acharonim are maskim." The note (15) shows that the Rema and Mishnah Berurah (281:3) limit the make-up obligation to לאל אשר שבת and that the Chayei Adam rules against skipping the piyut to daven with the tzibbur; but the body gave no pointer, so a reader of the body alone would take the Rav's statement as the practical halachah. Applied: a pointer in the body (Section VI), with the substance left in note 15.

Smaller points. (a) "YILC" and "KAJ" are unexplained abbreviations (as spoken); applied: marked as not explained on the recording. (b) The Rav's "Kel Odon" is the Ashkenazi pronunciation of אל אדון; applied: said once in Section II. (c) The Rav's AK-47 line to a named congregant and the name of the congregant in the war joke are omitted ("a particular congregant"); that is deliberate. (d) The Rav's list of days for the angels' creation in Section VIII ("the first day, before the first day, Monday ... the fourth day") is preserved as he said it and cross-referenced to note 5. (e) "Rav Naftali Yosef Ezra, a.k.a. Michael Zuckerman" and the jokes about Jewish names are kept.
