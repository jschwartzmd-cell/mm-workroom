Each shiur receives two reviews of different character:

Shiur Review — a content-focused read from the raw source material (transcript, summary, and finished write-up). Same in spirit as a chaver's reaction after sitting through it.

Editorial Review — a cold read of the finished write-up only, by a reviewer with no exposure to the source material or the writing process. Diagnostic — flags weak paragraphs, contradictions, factual concerns, copy-level polish suggestions. A friend who wasn't in the writing room.
