2026-09-06 — The Final מנחה, אֲחוֹת קְטַנָּה, and the Chofetz Chaim's Primordial Oath

Content-based review · reviewer read the transcript, summary, and finished write-up · 6 September 2026 · 24 Elul 5786 · yahrzeit of the חפץ חיים

1.The Shiur in One Paragraph

This is week two of the machzor arc, and it is a much better-organized shiur than last week's placeholder. The Rav opens on the יומא דהילולא of the חפץ חיים and uses that as the peg for a chiddush deep inside לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר, but he starts from an unusual place — not מעריב of Rosh HaShanah, but the מנחה that ends תשפ"ו. The unifying frame is הַכֹּל הוֹלֵךְ אַחַר הַחִתּוּם: how you close the old year is how the old year gets sealed. From there he moves through the Reb Yoel Gross / Skverer Rebbe מעשה about crying at בָּרֵךְ עָלֵינוּ הַשָּׁנָה הַזֹּאת in the final Mincha, then to the ספרדי transition-piyut אֲחוֹת קְטַנָּה of Rav Avraham Chazan (a bar-plugta/contemporary of the Ramban), then to the Arizal's סגולה of לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר and the Divine Name חת"ך hidden in פותח את ידך and in תבל ויושבי בה. That sets up the Chofetz Chaim's chiddush: ולא נשבע למרמה is the pre-natal oath of תהי צדיק ואל תהי רשע from נדה ל'. He then pivots to three halachic sugyos in the נוסח — שיר המעלות ממעמקים as הפסק between ישתבח and יוצר (with the דגל מרבבה's defense from the Rambam's testimony about the old ספרד minhag of אז ישיר), the זָכְרֵנוּ לְחַיִּים insertions and the לְחַיִּים/לַחַיִּים קפידא, and the origin of אבינו מלכנו in תענית כ"ה with the רבי עקיבא יסדו gematria. Closes with the Arizal on dancing on שמחת תורה as a סגולה for פרנסה.

2.What Was Strongest

  • Opening on the final מנחה instead of מעריב is a real move. Every year of shiurim on the machzor open with the first קדיש of the ליל ראש השנה. Starting with the last Mincha of the outgoing year, and building the whole opening around הַכֹּל הוֹלֵךְ אַחַר הַחִתּוּם, gives the mispallel something concrete to do at 6:47 pm Erev RH when he'd otherwise be checking his watch. This is the sort of frame-shift that changes behavior.
  • The Reb Yoel Gross / Skverer מעשה is perfectly-fit to the frame. Two links of מסורה (Reb Yoel → the previous Skverer Rebbe), one striking detail (crying specifically at בָּרֵךְ עָלֵינוּ הַשָּׁנָה הַזֹּאת), and a chiddush that generalizes (a tear at the last minute can pull the whole year up). That's a story doing structural work, not just חיזוק.
  • The Chofetz Chaim on ולא נשבע למרמה is the emotional and intellectual peak. The pshat is gorgeous: the pasuk asks who ascends הר ה', we all say "not me, I never swore falsely" — and the Chofetz Chaim reveals that everyone in the beis medrash swore, once, in utero, and the whole pasuk is about that oath. The full pull is that the Gemara in נדה itself brings the pasuk from לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר as the one who upheld the oath — so the C.C. is drawing out what's already latent in Chazal. That's exactly the Chofetz Chaim's דרך.
  • The חת"ך layer (פותח את ידך ends / תבל ויושבי בה begins) done together is a nice compact set-piece — two רמזים in different places converging on the same Name, tying לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר to the daily אשרי the mispaller already knows. Makes the Arizal's סגולה feel less מקובלים-esoteric and more textual.
  • The דגל מרבבה defense via the Rambam is a beautiful lomdish move. The Rav didn't just quote the נודע ביהודה — he brought the raiya. If the Rambam records אז ישיר as a legitimate old ספרד practice at the same מעבר, then the הפסק-issur cannot be about additions per se, only about שיחה בטלה. That's a real גזירה שוה-style ראיה, not a hand-wave.
  • The רבי עקיבא יסדו gematria on אבינו מלכנו חטאנו לפניך is one of those רמזים that once you hear it you can't unhear. The Rav framed it well — מחברים sign their piyutim; רבי עקיבא signed his in גימטריא instead of acrostic.
  • The לְחַיִּים vs. לַחַיִּים point — small, technical, but exactly the kind of practical קפידא a shul Rav should be teaching in the days before Rosh HaShanah. Concrete, actionable, and the מקובלים's לא חיים concern gives it emotional weight.

3.What Was Weaker or Worth Pushing Back On

The Prague Haskalah play was a fun aside but wasn't doing the work the Rav thinks it was. The setup is that הפסק at ישתבח-יוצר is as severe as עבירות שבידו that disqualify a soldier. The play then mocks that comparison. The Rav's punchline — "they didn't finish the play, the Chofetz Chaim and Reb Chaim won the war" — is charming but doesn't actually resolve the קלות ראש the story leaves in the air. Better to have simply cited the גמרא in סוטה where מעבירות שבידו is one shitah and הירא מן החטאים שבידו vs. actual fear-of-battle is a מחלוקת תנאים — that would have contained the maskilic gotcha halachically instead of anecdotally.

The three תירוצים on זָכְרֵנוּ לְחַיִּים were listed but not weighed. The Rav gave (1) צרכי ציבור, (2) Maharam's אופיין של הימים, and (3) חיים רוחניים qualified by למענך אלקים חיים — and then said "the strongest teretz is the third." But why is it strongest, and how do the other two interact with it? The first teretz is actually the most straightforward in Shulchan Aruch קי"ב — the איסור is on צרכי יחיד, not צרכי רבים — and it's not obvious the third is stronger halachically. A quick weighing of the three would have made the mispallel who forgot know which teretz to lean on when he can't restart.

The Vilna Gaon on זָכְרֵנוּ was passed off casually as "did not say" — that overstates the מעשה רב. The Gra's position is nuanced (he held they altered מטבע שטבעו חז"ל, and there are conflicting reports about whether he in fact refrained from saying them or said them בלית ברירה). Same overstatement crept into last week's shiur too. A "reportedly did not say them" would be more honest.

The אֲחוֹת קְטַנָּה attribution deserves a footnote of caution. The Rav names Rav Avraham Chazan and says he was a bar-plugta of the Ramban and delivered a hesped on him. The מחבר of the piyut is generally identified as Rav Avraham Chazan Girondi, but the specific link to a hesped for the Ramban is a מסורה among ספרדים and is not everywhere sourced. Not a mistake — just the sort of detail worth flagging as מסורת, not chronology.

The Bach's קשיא on לַחַיִּים was raised and then dropped without a real תירוץ. The Rav noted that והעמידנו מלכנו לחיים טובים in מעריב is said with a שוא (לְחַיִּים), which supposedly resolves the Bach's kasha — but the kasha stands only if the daily practice is with a פתח. The Rav essentially concedes the kasha is difficult. This is the place where saying "and I don't have a real teretz" would have been cleaner than the half-move.

The Arizal on שמחת תורה dancing as סגולה for פרנסה — landing at the end feels grafted on. It's a nice bracketing (both לְדָוִד מִזְמוֹר and שמחת תורה dancing = סגולה for פרנסה) but structurally it's the coda to a shiur about Rosh HaShanah נוסח, and שמחת תורה is a month away. Would have been sharper as a footnote or a "we'll get back to this."

4.Halachic / Lomdish Observations

On הכל הולך אחר החתימה and the final מנחה: the klal is well-known in הלכות ברכות (a wrong חתימה is מעכב in a way a wrong middle isn't). The Rav is doing a nice העברה from the halachic mechanism to the אגדתא of the last day of the year. A related move worth learning: the רמב"ן at the end of האמונה והביטחון (attributed) talks about the חתימת השנה in the same key. And the חזון איש (א"ח, on the ימים נוראים) discusses whether חתימה is a דין בזמן or a דין בפעולה — which changes what's happening in that final Mincha.

On the חפץ חיים's primordial oath — a genuine chiddush worth learning further: the Gemara in נדה ל' itself brings לא נשא לשוא נפשי ולא נשבע למרמה as the pasuk associated with the oath, so the C.C. is drawing out a Chazal-latent connection. This is essentially the same דרך the נפש החיים uses in שער א and the מסילת ישרים in the opening chapters — the תכלית was set before the נשמה came down, and the whole avodah is to remember it. Would be worth learning together with the Ramchal's דעת תבונות on why the נשמה forgets after birth if the goal is to remember.

On שיר המעלות as הפסק: the דגל מרבבה's raiya from the Rambam is one leg; there's a second, sharper leg in the poskim — the אשל אברהם (Butchatch) and later the ערוך השולחן נ"ד:ד who argue that שבחים are structurally part of the פסוקי דזמרה-to-יוצר flow, not an interruption of it. The Rav could have stacked those. Also worth flagging: the משנה ברורה in נ"ד is not fully with the דגל מרבבה — he brings the minhag but doesn't wholesale endorse the היתר, which is why נוסח אשכנז holds the line.

On the three תירוצים for זָכְרֵנוּ: the sharpest formulation of teretz #3 is that זָכְרֵנוּ לחיים is a בקשה that is a שבח — analogous to the שאלת גשמים in ברכת השנים being classified as גבורות-material by some Rishonim. That's a Brisker-style חילוק between בקשה בגדר בקשה and בקשה בגדר שבח. Worth pinning to the שולחן ערוך הרב in קי"ב.

On אבינו מלכנו corresponding to the middle ברכות: this is a well-known idea in the אבודרהם and the כל בו. Worth also learning the גר"א in שנות אליהו on אבינו מלכנו, who structures the paragraphs against specific themes rather than one-to-one against שמו"ע ברכות — a slightly different שיטה.

On the חת"ך רמזים: the more standard citation for the רמז in פותח את ידך is the של"ה הקדוש in מסכת תמיד (based on the כתבי האר"י); the רמז in תבל ויושבי בה is less standard and worth verifying (some editions of the כף החיים in קפ"ב and later collections have it, but it's not universally attested).

5.Follow-up Questions Worth Asking the Rav

Q1.If the final מנחה of תשפ"ו can retroactively elevate the whole year via בָּרֵךְ עָלֵינוּ הַשָּׁנָה הַזֹּאת — is that מרחק a חלות in the mispallel (like a קבלה that reframes past acts), a חלות in שמים (the גזר for the closing year is being re-signed), or is the Skverer Rebbe teaching a מנהג של רגש without a formal halachic mechanism?
Q2.The חפץ חיים's pshat that ולא נשבע למרמה = the pre-natal oath: does this displace the pshat of the ראשונים (רש"י, אב"ע, רד"ק) on שבועת שוא in the daily-life sense, or is it a דרך הדרוש layered on top of the pshat? And is the חפץ חיים in his printed works or is this an oral מסורה?
Q3.On שיר המעלות — is the Rav paskening it for our shul, or is he presenting both sides descriptively? Given the shul's נוסח, what's the actual halacha l'ma'aseh for the mispallel this Rosh HaShanah?
Q4.On לְחַיִּים/לַחַיִּים: if a mispallel said לַחַיִּים b'shogeg, does he go back? The קפידא in the מקובלים that מקטרגים can mishear as לֹא חַיִּים — is that a דין or a חשש? And if a Yid is בעל קרי-fluent in לַחַיִּים from daily מעריב, is there a practical eitzah to avoid the tongue-slip on Rosh HaShanah?
Q5.On the רבי עקיבא יסדו gematria — which of the ראשונים brings it? Is this an אבודרהם, a כל בו, a מחזור ויטרי, or a later collection? The rov מחברים who signed piyutim did so in acrostic; a gematria-signature is unusual enough to deserve a source.
Q6.On the Arizal's סגולה for פרנסה via שמחת תורה dancing — does the Rav hold this is a סגולה in the שמחה itself (i.e. any Yid on any שמחת תורה can plug in), or is it specifically the Arizal-style כוונה that pulls the שפע? The distinction matters for the ba'al habayis who dances without knowing what the Arizal says.

6.Bottom Line

This is a much tighter shiur than last week's opener. The Rav had time to prep, and it shows: seven distinct sugyos, each with a defined halachic/aggadic move, held together by a real thematic spine (opening/closing the year, the נוסח of the day, and one deep chiddush from the yahrzeit-baal-shem). The חפץ חיים's primordial-oath pshat is the takeaway and it earns its place — it's the kind of chiddush that reorganizes how a mispallel reads a whole קאפיטל תהילים. The Skverer story and the דגל מרבבה raiya from the Rambam are the two other set-pieces that will stay with the oilam. Weaknesses are mostly of the "raised-and-dropped" kind — the three תירוצים on זָכְרֵנוּ weren't weighed, the Bach's קשיא was left hanging, the Vilna Gaon was overstated, and the שמחת תורה coda felt bolted on. Nothing that undermines the shiur; things a good chavrusa would ask about after. As the second of three shiurim on the machzor, this one lands — the Rav has moved from "orientation" (last week) to "specific חלקי התפילה," and set up next week's promised deeper dive into פיוטים and their מחברים.
