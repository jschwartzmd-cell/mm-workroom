Mareh Mekamos · Shiur Review

2026-09-07 — כ"ה אלול, ראש השנה שחל בשבת, and the פיוטים Machloikes

Content-based review · reviewer read the transcript, summary, and finished write-up · 7 September 2026 · 25 Elul 5786 · yahrzeit of ר' אלעזר בר' שמעון

1.The Shiur in One Paragraph

This is the third and last of the machzor-prep shiurim — an extra Labor Day sitting jammed in because next Sunday will be swallowed by יום טוב. The Rav opens on the significance of כ"ה אלול (Rebbi Eliezer's date for בראשית; Nechemiah's חומת ירושלים; the Zohar in בלק on the fall of מלך רומי), signals the yahrzeit of ר' אלעזר בר' שמעון that will become load-bearing at the end, and then does "raid bites" in no particular order: the Pshevarsker-Rebbe pshat on הרוגים על שם קדשך vs. טבוחים על יחודך anchored in the Danube ma'aseh; the halachic problem of ראש השנה שחל בשבת with the Pri Megadim's ספק on fasting and the Minchas Elazar/oiberlender-bochur maaseh on drinking coffee; the machloikes about crying on Shabbos-RH (רפ"ח, בשמך יגילון = בכיה, and the Reshis Chochma's tear-on-the-forehead); sleeping (מאן דדמיך בריש שתא, יושב בטל כישן דמי); the seven-fold למנצח לבני קרח and the Aruch HaShulchan's warning about hefsek at תקיעת שופר; התרת נדרים with the מסירת מודעא tip and the גמרא in נדרים כ"ג; the שליח ציבור's המלך from his place as a national coronation; and the shiur's climax — the Firqoi ben Baboi/Cairo genizah teshuvah from Rav Yehudai Gaon that פיוטים originated as a substitute for davenen under a גזירת שמד, and the resulting battle between the geonim (Rav Hai, Rav Saadya, the Rosh, and the Gra) who wanted them out, and the Maharil who — after his daughter died the year he removed one — locked down the minhag. Closes on the machloikes about the identity of ר' אלעזר הקליר, Rabbeinu Tam's Aruch-based ma'aseh with the cake-and-קמיע, and the "fire burning around him" tradition.

2.What Was Strongest

  • The Firqoi ben Baboi setup is the intellectual centerpiece and it lands. The Rav names the source, names the רבי (Rav Yehudai Gaon), names how we know about it (the Cairo genizah — inaccessible to Rabi Akiva Eiger, the Ketzos, the Arizal, all pre-1897), and then draws the sharp historical arc: piyutim as substitute-davenen under a גזירת שמד; the Muslim conquest removing the gezeirah; the geonim asking why we still say them; the ashkenaz-vs-בבל machloikes where this time eretz yisrael won. That's a full, honest historical picture of how the machzor came to be — the kind of thing most shul Ravs skip.
  • The Pshevarsker Rebbe's הרוגים/טבוחים pshat is exquisitely-fit to אבינו מלכנו. Two lines that most mispallelim skim as a doublet, and one image — the shot moving right-to-left down the Danube line, some finishing אחד and some not — separates them. The Rav's honest addition ("before October 7 I thought of my great-grandfather; now I think of all of them") gives it kavanah-fuel that will actually change how a mispallel says those two lines this year.
  • The Minchas Elazar / oiberlender-bochur maaseh is a perfect halachic anecdote. It's not sentiment — it's psak encoded in story. The mesirus-nefesh chumrah of not-drinking-before-davenen is real; the איסור דאורייתא of fasting on Shabbos past חצות is bigger. The Minchas Elazar himself carrying in the tray with coffee, milk, and sugar cubes for the makpid bochur is the picture that will stick.
  • The בשמך יגילון = ראשי תיבות בכיה is a lovely compact רמז. Placed exactly where the shiur is pivoting from "you can't cry on Shabbos" to "on Rosh Hashanah the neshamah senses אימת הדין," it does structural work — it plants a מקור for tears inside the שופר-קדושה itself.
  • The Aruch HaShulchan's warning on למנצח vs. תקיעת שופר is real posek-mode. "You're being מבטל a מצות עשה דאורייתא for a מנהג from the Arizal — know your priorities." Sharp, unsentimental, and the note that the old Rödelheim/Heidenheim machzorim don't even have the seven-fold למנצח is a beautiful supporting raiya from history.
  • The מסירת מודעא tip on התרת נדרים is exactly the kind of "small practical" a shul Rav should be teaching. Say the מסירת מודעא לנדרים דלהבא at home; save the beis din three minutes. And the framing of "if you owe money to a shul, pay it — don't rely on התרה" is the mussar the halacha demands.
  • The Maharil ma'aseh (removed a piyut, daughter died, retracted) is one of those stories that closes the argument. It converts the abstract "don't mess with minhag" into a concrete קבלה for the mispallel. The Rav gets the timing right — he lets the whole geonic critique breathe first, then drops the Maharil as the closing wall.

3.What Was Weaker or Worth Pushing Back On

The Firqoi/Rav Yehudai attribution needs a caveat. The Rav framed the Firqoi ben Baboi documents as revealing Rav Yehudai Gaon's position via his talmid. Academically this is contested — some scholars read Firqoi as an independent author with his own polemical agenda who invokes Rav Yehudai's authority rather than transmitting a verified teshuvah. The bottom line (geonim were unhappy with piyutim) is well-attested from Rav Hai and Rav Saadya themselves; the specific Firqoi-as-transmitter-of-Rav-Yehudai claim is a scholarly reconstruction and deserves the "the academics say" framing.

The "ashkenaz vs. bavel — this time eretz yisrael won" line is a rhetorical flourish that doesn't quite fit. The מנהג to say piyutim entered ashkenaz through the חסידי אשכנז and via אמצע-הרישונים chains (Kalonymus family, etc.), not through a direct eretz-yisrael-beat-bavel victory. The historical picture is subtler: bavel did win halachically almost everywhere, and piyut is one of the few places where the ashkenaz מסורה — itself downstream of eretz yisrael through the Kalonymus line — kept a non-bavel practice alive. Would have been cleaner to just say that.

The Vilna Gaon on יוצרות was flattened. The Rav said the Gra held it was "halachically illegal" full stop. The Gra's actual position is more articulated: some piyutim yes, some no, and specifically inside ברכות קריאת שמע and inside שמו"ע is where the hefsek concern sharpens. And, importantly, the Gra's own קהילה-level psak was implemented differently in different places (Vilna vs. the various nuschaos of מעשה רב). Same tendency showed up in last week's shiur — the Gra keeps getting reduced to a single crisp position.

The בשמך יגילון pivot from "no crying on Shabbos" to "yes crying on RH" bypassed the Shabbos-RH combination itself. The shiur was framed around Rosh HaShanah being on Shabbos this year, but the crying discussion resolved into a general RH answer without weighing the Shabbos overlay. Does the אריז"ל's permission for uncontrolled tears from אימת הדין get muted when it's Shabbos? Does the Reshis Chochma tear-on-forehead move happen when it's Shabbos? The Rav opened the door and walked through it without answering.

The קליר = ר' אלעזר בר' שמעון problem was raised and then abandoned mid-sentence. The Rav noticed the internal contradiction — if the Kalir is a Tanna, then the geonim's whole "piyutim are a post-חז"ל hefsek" critique collapses. This is a huge kasha and the Rav essentially said "so some rishonim hold he was later." But which rishonim, and how the Rabbeinu-Tam-vs.-later-rishonim machloikes maps onto the geonic-critique problem, is exactly what should have been sharpened. Half a page of Tos. in חגיגה י"ג would have carried the punch.

The cake/קמיע/fire-around-him ma'aseh from the ערוך is at the edge of what a shul-Rav-in-2026 can say without framing. The Rav did protect it — "it sounds like a ba'al shem-te maaseh, but the Aruch and Rabbeinu Tam say this, these were holy Jews, when they wrote these things they were b'ruach hakodesh." That's the right disclaimer. But given the audience (Dr., R' Avish, etc.) it would have been cleaner to note that Rabbeinu Tam explicitly says he heard it איש מפי איש from Rashi through R' Meir — the mesorah-chain, not the miracle detail, is what carries the halachic weight for the Kalir-as-Tanna position.

The מילה דוחה שבת analogy for crying-as-מילת הלב was dismissed too quickly. The Rav's counter — "it's מילה שלא בזמנה, you should have done teshuvah yesterday" — is clever but doesn't hold. מילת הלב is not a fixed-day חיוב like בשמיני; it's a קיום whose זמן is precisely the day the neshamah is being judged. The person who raised it had a real סברא and the Rav had the room to say "I don't have a good תירוץ but the halacha of no-crying stands" instead of the half-move.

4.Halachic / Lomdish Observations

On איסור תענית בשבת past חצות: the classical formulation is in שו"ע אורח חיים רפ"ח. But there's a real machloikes in the אחרונים whether the איסור is דאורייתא (a violation of עונג שבת which is itself דאורייתא per the Rambam and the Ramban) or דרבנן (a takanah). The Rav called it איסור מן התורה; the Mishnah Berurah is more careful — see רפ"ח:ז where the analysis of תענית חלום exceptions suggests it's not straightforwardly דאורייתא. Matters for the סברא-weight against drinking before davenen.

On the Pri Megadim's ספק: the sharpest formulation is whether תענית requires קבלת תענית (subjective) or שיעור אכילה-absence (objective). If the former, drinking anything with intent to break the תענית works; if the latter, only a שיעור פת כביצה (or כותבת per yom kippur analogy) works. Worth pairing with the מנחת חינוך on מצות שביתה whether שביתה is a קיום or a שלילה.

On למנצח שבע פעמים and hefsek: the Aruch HaShulchan's warning is one leg; there's a second, sharper leg in the poskim. The משנה ברורה in תקצ"ב lays out that hefsek between the ברכה and the fulfillment is treated with the strictness of hefsek between ברכת המצוות and מצוה generally — an איסור דרבנן that has real teeth, up to and including whether one needs to re-say the ברכה. The Rav could have stacked those.

On the גמרא נדרים כ"ג — הרוצה שלא יתקיימו נדריו: the interaction with כל נדרי at the start of יום כיפור is a whole sugya. Some Rishonim (the Rosh among them) tie the כל נדרי נוסח explicitly to this גמרא — נדרים שאני עתיד לידור — while Rabbeinu Tam famously argues כל נדרי is about past nedarim and rewrote the נוסח accordingly. This is the piece of context that would have connected the Rav's ערב-RH מסירת מודעא to the erev-YK practice for the same מספלל.

On the Firqoi/geonic-critique geography: the sharpest lomdish move is that the geonim's target was specifically פיוטים inside ברכות שמו"ע and inside ברכות קריאת שמע (יוצרות), not piyutim in חזרת הש"ץ at neutral moments or piyutim before/after שמו"ע. That's why the Gra's operational move (many piyutim yes, but reposition them out of the ברכות themselves) works — it addresses the actual halachic concern rather than the surface-level "are piyutim allowed." Worth pinning to שו"ע או"ח ס"ח and the בית יוסף ad loc.

On the Kalir-as-Tanna question: Rabbeinu Tam's identification in תוס' חגיגה י"ג rests on linguistic and rhetorical grounds (the Kalir's Hebrew has tannaitic-register features). The counter-position (later — סבוראים or early גאונים-era eretz yisrael) rests on the presence of names/dates/references that postdate the Tannaim. Worth learning the שיבולי הלקט and the ר' יצחק אבן גיאת for the pre-modern debate; the modern academic identification (6th-7th c. eretz yisrael) is downstream of the Cairo genizah paytanic corpus and, notably, converges with the Rav Yehudai / Firqoi timing the Rav laid out.

5.Follow-up Questions Worth Asking the Rav

Q1.On בכיה on RH-that-is-Shabbos specifically: does the Rav pasken like the אריז"ל (uncontrolled tears from אימת הדין — permitted) or does the Shabbos overlay tighten the standard? And is the Reshis Chochma tear-on-forehead סגולה done on Shabbos, or does that become a מלאכה-חשש (rubbing) that's dropped this year?
Q2.On the Firqoi-ben-Baboi teshuvah: is the Rav aware of the academic pushback that Firqoi may be an independent author invoking Rav Yehudai's authority rather than transmitting a verified teshuvah? Does that change the משקל of the argument that the geonim wanted piyutim gone, or is the Rav Hai / Rav Saadya evidence-base strong enough independently that the Firqoi document is additional support rather than the support?
Q3.On the Kalir-as-ר' אלעזר בר' שמעון problem: Rabbeinu Tam's קבלה איש מפי איש is a formidable chain (Rashi → R' Meir → Rabbeinu Tam). If we accept it, the geonim's whole "piyutim came from a post-חז"ל gezeirah" critique collapses. How does the Rav resolve this — with the Rabbeinu-Tam-was-mistaken position, or with the "ר' אלעזר בר' שמעון wrote some piyutim but Kalir the paytan is a later figure who shares the name" reading?
Q4.On the שליח ציבור beginning המלך from his place: is this Rav's מסורה from a specific chassidishe rebbe, or is it a widespread minhag with a printed source? And if it's a minhag, is a shatz who was raised in a non-chassidishe shul supposed to adopt it, or is it a מנהג המקום question?
Q5.On the מסירת מודעא-at-home tip: the Rav suggested doing it privately after the בית דין to save time. But the reason it's traditionally done in front of שלושה is not just for התרה — for מסירת מודעא there's a שיטה that עדים are constitutive of the מודעא itself. Is the Rav relying on the שיטה that מסירת מודעא doesn't need עדים when it's about future נדרים as opposed to גיטין and קידושין?
Q6.On piyutim more broadly: given the Rav's clear preference for פסוקי דזמרה / קר"ש / שמו"ע over piyutim, what's the actual instruction to the mispallel this year in our shul? Say the piyutim quickly and give the core davenen the time? Or skip specific piyutim (which ones?) and use the time for מוסף-kavanos? Concrete הנהגה would translate the shiur's halachic frame into practice.

6.Bottom Line

This is the strongest of the three machzor-prep shiurim. The Rav had a real thesis (piyutim are a גזירת שמד-era substitute that never got extracted, and the whole geonim-vs-Maharil battle is downstream of that fact), a real anchor מסורה from the Cairo genizah, and a real בעל-הבית Judaism at stake — "on ראש השנה we're going to focus on the core davenen, and the piyutim are extra." The Pshevarsker Rebbe's הרוגים/טבוחים pshat is the emotional peak and is placed early; the Firqoi ben Baboi/Kalir arc is the intellectual peak and closes the shiur. In between, the practical הלכות for RH-on-Shabbos (fast/coffee, crying, sleeping, שופר hefsek, נדרים) are all covered with real anecdotes and specific psak. Weaknesses are of the "raised-and-dropped" and "flattened a nuanced position" kind — the Kalir-as-Tanna kasha wasn't sharpened, the Vilna Gaon got compressed to a soundbite, and the Shabbos-overlay on בכיה was left open. Nothing that undermines the shiur; things a good chavrusa would ask about after. As the final pre-RH shiur, this one earns its place: the oilam walks out with three concrete instructions (drink coffee, don't say למנצח once tekiot start, do מסירת מודעא at home), one real chiddush of understanding (piyutim came from a gezeirah), and one framework for kavanah at the two hardest lines of אבינו מלכנו.
